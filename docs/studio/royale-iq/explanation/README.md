> *Understanding-oriented.* Why Royale IQ exists and where it sits in the umbrella.

## Why pro bono, why this niche

Royale IQ sits in the **influencers** niche of [Studio Co.](../../co/) — creators treated as a
company. It is offered **pro bono**: its strategic value is not direct revenue but proving and
showcasing the [Agent AI](../../../engine/agent-ai/) core in a real, engaging consumer product.

## Engine first, product second

Royale IQ is the first **product** built on Agent AI. The engine is designed to power more AI
products over time; Royale IQ is the proving ground. This mirrors the Press → Studio Press path:
**OGS Engine builds the core, OGS Studio packages it.**

## Why a game app

Clash Royale gives a well-defined, data-rich domain (player profiles, decks, matchups) that
exercises the agent's reasoning workflow (retrieve → analyze → coach) end to end — a focused way to
validate Agent AI before broader use.
