OGS tech (legal entity **Odenir Gomes Solutions**, CNPJ 54.705.657/0001-83, São Paulo/SP)
is defined by four official statements. Each is bilingual by principle — PT · EN is how the
brand thinks, not a courtesy translation. The visual identity exists to sustain these
statements — see [Visual Identity](visual-identity.md) for the brand assets.

---

## The official statements

### 01 · Mission
> **Technology that takes your business further.**
> *Tecnologia que leva o seu negócio além.*

We deliver technology solutions that unlock each client's next level — operational,
commercial, and strategic.

### 02 · Purpose
> **Tecnologia de qualidade não deveria ser privilégio de empresa grande.**
> *Quality technology shouldn't be a privilege reserved for big companies.*

Democratize access to enterprise-class solutions for Brazilian small and medium businesses (SMBs).

### 03 · Vision
> **Ser a maior empresa de tecnologia pra pequenas e médias empresas do Brasil.**
> *To become Brazil's largest technology company for small and medium businesses.*

Lead through the combination of technical depth, closeness to the client, and operational consistency.

### 04 · Values
> **We lead with ethics, we grow with people.**
> *Lideramos com ética, crescemos com pessoas.*

Non-negotiable ethics in every decision. People first — in teams, in partners, in clients.
And genuinely bilingual: Portuguese and English are how the brand thinks, hires, and
documents.

## The brand concept

The OGS mark is **"O Avião"** — a paper plane climbing forward and up, the way OGS takes
each business *further*:

- **Solid silhouette** — a filled shape, not an outline. One color already works, from
  favicon to billboard, on any background.
- **The wing is the signature** — one brand color carries the structure; the **amber wing**
  is the accent (≤ ¼ of the area), the same amber of the `tech` descriptor in the wordmark.
- **Geometry, not freehand** — four points and one crease make the drawing reproducible at
  any scale. Fixed attitude: climbing at ~28°, never level, never diving.
