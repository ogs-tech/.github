## Anatomy

The symbol has two elements:

- **Globe** — central wireframe: vertical meridians + a 3D equator + the **S wave**.
- **Tricolor ring** — three external arcs with symmetric gaps (top, bottom-right, bottom-left).
  - **Green arc** · reach
  - **Amber arc** · presence
  - **Blue arc** · trust

| Part | Role |
|---|---|
| **Wordmark** | `OGS` — Space Grotesk · 600 · letter-spacing 0.04em |
| **Tagline** | `Technology · Brazil` — JetBrains Mono · 400 · letter-spacing 0.20em · uppercase |

---

## Construction & grid

Built on a **10×10 grid** with symmetric arc gaps every **120°**.

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Globe | 0.68R (68% of area) |
| Ring | 0.89R |
| Stroke base | 2.2% |
| Arc / gap | 100° / 20° |

## Variations & lockups

Three primary variations and two lockups. Use the version with the best contrast for the context.

| Variation | Use |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | light backgrounds |
| **Dark** | dark backgrounds |
| **Mono** | embroidery, signage |

- **Horizontal** lockup — primary
- **Vertical** lockup — secondary

---

## Scale & clear space

Recommended sizes: 180 / 120 / 80 / 48 / 32 / 24 px.

- **Minimum size:** 24px (digital) · 8mm (print). Below that, use the isolated `OGS` wordmark.
- **Clear space (rule X):** minimum breathing room around the mark equals **X**, where X is the
  height of the wordmark's letter "O". No other graphic element may enter that area.

## Color

Six-token palette. Green, amber, and blue are a modern reinterpretation of the Brazilian flag —
more sophisticated and fit for a tech context.

| Token | Role | HEX | RGB |
|---|---|---|---|

## Typography

Two families compose the system.

| Family | Use | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Space Grotesk** | wordmark & body text | weights 400 / 500 / 600 / 700; geometric, modern, technical personality |
| **JetBrains Mono** | technical details & uppercase taglines | weight 400; e.g. `TECHNOLOGY · BRAZIL` |

## Official seal

A clean vector insignia attesting OGS Tech provenance: the tricolor reduced to two lateral dots
(green + blue), the central globe without rings, and mono typography around it. Applied to official
materials, proposals, packaging, and digital identity.

| Variant | Use |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | standard on all material |
| **Dark · Ink background** | dark surfaces |

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Double ring | 2.2pt + 0.7pt |
| Lateral dots | Green · Blue |
| Central globe | mono · no rings |
| Minimum | 40px / 14mm |

## Stamp

The physical object — single-ink pressed on paper, with the irregularity of a manual application.
Used to authenticate contracts, receipts, proposals, and correspondence.

- **Colors:** blue, black, red, green.
- **Utility stamps** (rectangular, same DNA): `PAGO`, `APROVADO`, `CONFIDENCIAL`, `CONFORME`.
- **Rule:** always applied at an angle (−15° to +5°). Do not fix the imperfection — it is part of
  the identity. *(Opacity 0.92 · grain base-freq 0.55.)*

---

## Misuse — do not

To protect recognition, never:

- ✕ Deform the mark
- ✕ Recolor it
- ✕ Rotate it
- ✕ Add effects
- ✕ Place it on low-contrast backgrounds
- ✕ Swap the wordmark
