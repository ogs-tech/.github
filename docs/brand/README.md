> **Your business. Further. Future-Ready.** — the OGS tech brand identity.

This section holds the **brand identity** of OGS tech (Odenir Gomes Solutions), distilled
from the **Guia de Marca, Ed. 03**. It complements the
[Brand Architecture](../explanation/brand-architecture.md) — which explains the *business* umbrella
(Partners · Studio · Engine) — by covering the *identity*: who the company is and how the mark looks.

> **Source:** [Guia de Marca, Ed. 03](https://dx05qx18l5va6.cloudfront.net/ogs-tech/brand/) — the
> live brand book, published from the [`brand`](https://github.com/ogs-tech/brand) repository.

| Page | Diátaxis | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| [Company](company.md) | 💡 Explanation | Mission, purpose, vision, values, slogan, brand concept |
| [Visual Identity](visual-identity.md) | 📖 Reference | The plane symbol, construction, color ladder, typography, misuse |

> **Brand architecture vs. brand identity.** [Brand Architecture](../explanation/brand-architecture.md)
> answers *how the business is structured*; this section answers *what the brand is and looks like*.
> They cross-link rather than duplicate.
